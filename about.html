About

My name is Francisco, and since my childhood in Spain in the 80s, I have been interested in Japanese animation without knowing it, watching Heidi, Marco, Mazinger Z, Battle of the Planets and many other co-productions made by Japanese and European production companies.

In the 90s, Ranma 1/2 was one of the animes that made me think this is different, but at that time there was no internet and no way to get more information about the genre.
At the same time, Saint Seiya, Dragon Ball, Dr. Slump, and later Rurouni Kenshin, Akira, and Ghost in the Shell sparked more interest, and with the appearance of the internet, the doors opened for me.

In ' 97, I started to buy manga, at first in Spanish, and later in English . Soon after, I collected figures, action figures, and model kits ; in '05-'06, I got some cels and shikishis, and 30 years later, I have acquired a collection to go with the memories of reading and watching some anime that are still part of myself.
Now I want to share (and collect information) about the little pieces that make up the last (and next) 30 years of this hobby.

Thanks, all!
