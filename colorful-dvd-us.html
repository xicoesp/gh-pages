Colorful! - DVD - US

By Xico
July 15, 2026
This is a fun one. I don't remember why and where I got this DVD, probably I got it from the Archonia webstore in the mid 2000s, and boy, what an anime.

This is a collection of short, bizarre stories (16 episodes of 7 minutes) of some boys and men trying to see some panties. The animation is a mix of over-the-top expressions and realistic imagery, with some weird angles and colors .

The edition is from ADV, region 1 (USA), 110 minutes  and has Japanese audio with subtitles and English dub.
