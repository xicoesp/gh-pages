Tokyo Tribe 2 - Santa Inoue

By Xico
August 21, 2026

This continuation of the single volume Tokyo Tribe is know also as Tokyo Tribes, is a manga of Santa Inoue and was serialized in the magazine Boom from 1997 to 2005 collected in 12 volumes.

This title has a manga spin-off and continuations (Tokyo Tribe 2: Spin Off!, Tokyo Tribe 3 and Tokyo Tribe Waru) an anime with 13 episodes and a live actiion adaptation.

This particular volume is the 3td of the Tokyo Tribe 2 series, and it has Santa Inoue signature and a simple illustration of the main character, Kai.
