Ranma 1/2 - PT (1996)

By Xico
July 15, 2026

With this first post about manga, and I want to start with the first manga I bought, Ranma 1/2. This year mark 30 years since that time, in September of 1997 when it came to the stands in Portugal, the first number of a +/- 32 pages edition of this title by Rumiko Takahashi.

It was a comic format edition in size, with the pages inverted to occidental reading, and it was short lived, with only 12 numbers published.

I already knew the title as the anime was aired in Spain in 1993 while I lived there, and it was my go to watch before I went to high school. The humor, fighting and "romance" made me a fan of this title, and later of other works by Rumiko Takahashi.
