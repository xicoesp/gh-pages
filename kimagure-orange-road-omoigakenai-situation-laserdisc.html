Kimagure Orange Road: Omoigakenai Situation - LaserDisc

By Xico
September 22, 2026

I´m a sucker for 80s rom-coms, and Kimagure Orange Road is an excelent example. Classic character design, good balance between comedy and romance, a sprincle of fantasy (some characters have "powers") and an excelent OST.

I don´t have LaserDisc player, but the art of the cover of the first episode of the 3rd volume of the OVAs, released on 1991, was enough to put this piece in my collection.
