Manga Original Art - The good, the bad, the expensive.

By Xico
September 15, 2026

Collecting original art related to manga its a world on it own, most of the american comics related to the big franchises of Marvel and DC, have there artists hired by the publisher to work on their proprety, and they dont "own" the characters, even if they create a new one. In the manga universe, if a mangaka create a new series, they are owners of the characters and story, and they have some sentimental atachment and cultural obligation to preserve the original, and thats one of the reasons for the low quantity of original manga art sold.

I dont say there is no original manga art in the hands of collectors and sold in marletplaces (i own hundreds), but the vast maiority is from decesed authors, retired, or gifted as prices.

Retired/deseced authors.

Most of the original art circulating the market are on this category. The heirs of the author, to make some money from propieties that are less know, sell the original manuscripts, some times to a dealer, and then apear on marketplaces. On other cases, the manuscripts are "left" on the publisher and sold by some employee when there is no one to care for them.

Retired authors also sell some original art to complement income, but selling your own original working pages was sometimes viewed as a personal shame or a sign of financial failure, but this is changing and some authors are more willing to sell their art.

Stolen Art.

Sadly some art is stoled from the authors. Leiji Matsumoto, Naoko Takeuchi, Osamu Tezuka and in 2025 Masakazu Katsura are some of the creators that had original art stolen, from outsiders, publishser employes or assistants. Some of this art was recovered, other still at large.

Fake/forgeries.

When the deal is too good....

Some forgeries had apear on the market, as original, or listed as "hand made manuscrit from the series but not autentificated". Pages from Katsuhiro Otomo´s Akira, Go Nagai´s Devilman, Osamu Tezuka´s Balckjack and Goseki Kojima´s Lone Wolf & Cub are some of the know fakes listed and sold some years ago, and I saw pieces of Kuramada´s Saint Seiya and Rumiko´s Maison Ikkoku for sale that had all the signs of a forgery.

Its not easy to verify the autenticity of a piece, knowing the creation process, the materials used, the seller reputation, providence of the piece and using the published page to do some "forensics" are some of the steps to at least have some certain of the autenticity, but common sense is your friend, if you see a One Piece original manga page .... run!
