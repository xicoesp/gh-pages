Legend of Basara - DVD - JP

By Xico
September 3, 2026

When a client told me he had a business trip to Japan and asked if I wanted something from there, I told him to get me one or two anime DVDs. it was the early 00s, and at that time I worked in a video rental/shop. I was curious and want to have a Japanese anime DVD in my collection. A couple of weeks later apear with 2 DVDs, one of them was Legend of Basara vol.1, a shoujo with fantasy elements based on a manga (Basara) by Yumi Tamura and published in English by Viz from 2003 to 2008.

The price of the DVD was 4180 yen, which at that time was something like 33$, I watched once and it has been in my collection since.
