Lupin III Assembled Figure Collection (Banpresto)

By Xico
September 3, 2026

I discovered Lupin III by chance, one night with my friends drinking beer in a bar, and then I saw on the tv an anime show and asked myself "An anime at this hour?". At the time, it was common to show hentai late at night on one of the channels, but other friendlier types of anime were reserved for the children´s slot, and the fact that the second channel of the public television chose to air this show at that time made me curious.

This set of four prize figures makes a diorama recreating scenes from the second and most famous season of this show, featuring  Lupin III, Daisuke Jigen, Goemon Ishikawa and Fujiko Mine.
