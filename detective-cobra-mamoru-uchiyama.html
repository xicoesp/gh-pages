Detective Cobra - Mamoru Uchiyama

By Xico
September 1, 2026

Mamoru Uchiyama is best known for his numerous manga based on the Ultraman series, but for almost 20 years he created and illustrated mangas of different genres, and one of them was "Detective Cobra", a crime manga published in Weekly Manga Goraku between 1993 and 1995.

This chapter front page is for the chapter 2 of volume 2, and resembles the typical action movie poster of the 90s, like Lethal Weapon or Hard to Kill, with the main character holding his weapon.
