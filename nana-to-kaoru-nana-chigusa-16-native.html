Nana to Kaoru - Nana Chigusa 1/6 (Native)

By Xico
July 15, 2026
Do you like S&M? Do you like romcoms? This is the main character of the manga (OVA and live-action movie) Nana to Kaoru, by Ryuta Amazume, a comedic romance revolving around the relationship of Kaoru Sugimura, a 17-year-old virgin with an S&M fetish, and Nana Chigusa, her childhood friend and neighbor.

This astonishing figure made by Native was released in 2011, and it has two versions, one in black (the one I have) and one in red. The two versions retailed for 9,000 yen and were sold in limited quantities.

To me, it is one of the most beautiful figures I have, very accurate to the manga, with great sculpting, vibrant colors, and fantastic paint.
